Shipping & Returns

Shipping from our studio to you.

Physical crystal orders ship from China to the United States. Personal energy profile reports are delivered online.

Processing time

Physical crystal orders are prepared and packed in about 3 business days before shipment.

Ships from China

Our crystal jewelry is fulfilled from China and shipped to customers in the United States.

Estimated delivery

After processing, most U.S. deliveries arrive in about 7-15 business days. Timing can vary during holidays, customs checks, or carrier delays.

Physical crystal orders

Please allow about 3 business days for order preparation before your package is handed to the carrier. This preparation time is separate from transit time.

Once your order ships, delivery to the United States usually takes about 7-15 business days. In some cases, customs processing, weather, holidays, carrier volume, or address issues may extend delivery.

If tracking is available for your order, we will send it to the email address used at checkout. Please make sure your shipping address is complete and accurate before placing your order.

Personal energy profile reports

Personal energy profile reports are digital products. They do not require physical shipping and will not be sent as a package.

After you complete the required questions, your free preview is generated online. If you choose to unlock the full report, the complete report is generated digitally and made available through your report link. A notification email may also be sent to the email address provided at checkout.

Delivery issues

If your package appears delayed, damaged, or delivered to the wrong address, contact us with your order number and any available tracking details.

For returns and refunds, please review our Refund Policy.

Returns

If a return is needed, we will provide instructions before you send anything back. Please do not send items back without contacting us first.

In many cases, especially for damaged, incorrect, delayed, or unsatisfactory orders, we may resolve the issue without requiring a return.
